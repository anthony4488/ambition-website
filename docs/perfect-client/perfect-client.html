Onboarding: read before your first session

The Ambition athlete.
What we look for, and what to aim for.

Every athlete we take on is chosen. This is the athlete, and the family, that gets the most out of this programme. Read it before you start, and hold yourself to it from week one.

01 The athlete

  • 13 to 24, already on a high-level pathway. Academy, rep, state league, semi-pro or professional, in any sport.
  • Wants it themselves. Not their parent's idea. They want to play at the highest level in their sport, and they know being a bit better isn't enough.
  • Already works hard. They're here for the edge, not a rescue. Effort isn't the problem; direction is.
  • Coachable. Takes a cue, tries it, and asks why. Questions are welcome. Excuses aren't.
  • Reads their own numbers. Every session is timed and reviewed. The best athletes know their times and want to beat them.

02 How they train with us

  • At every session, on time, ready. Boots, water, warm and switched on. Missed sessions are the one thing no programme can fix.
  • Does the work in between. What we set for the days away from us gets done, and done properly.
  • Straight with us. Tells us about sleep, soreness, niggles, match minutes and club training load, before the session, not after it.
  • Quality over volume. Every rep is fast and measured. A tired rep that's slower than the last one isn't extra work; it's the wrong work.
  • In the WhatsApp group. Sessions, numbers and changes are shared there. Read it.

03 The family

  • Answers the phone and turns up when they say they will.
  • Gives straight answers about where the athlete really is: level, schedule, injuries, goals.
  • Supports, doesn't coach from the sideline. The athlete owns the work. You back it.
  • Thinks long term. This is a two year build, not a ten week course. Most athletes stay two years or more.

What to aim for

→ Your first block

  • Never miss a session.
  • Learn your numbers: your times, and what each test tells you.
  • Know your limiter by name, from your assessment, and why it's first.
  • Do every piece of work set between sessions.
  • Tell us your weekly club and match schedule, every week.

→ Your first year

  • Train the priorities from your report, in order.
  • Chase the 12 month targets on each one.
  • Beat your own numbers, session by session. The only comparison that matters is you against last time.
  • Show the speed on game day, not just on the gates.

Not the right fit

  • Wants a quick fix before trials in three weeks.
  • Is comparing us with the cheapest session in Sydney.
  • Wants a guarantee before committing. We guarantee the measuring and the honesty, never a number, a contract or a selection.
  • Is "just looking", or is only here because a parent booked it.
